厚涂幻想风
Painterly Fantasy Anime Style

特点：画面质感强烈、类似油画或游戏。
A silver-haired mage stands on the edge of a floating island, wearing a deep-blue robe and a flowing cloak billowing in the wind. In his right hand, he holds a staff tipped with a glowing crystal, while his left hand is raised as if casting a spell. The sky behind him is a vast mix of deep blue stars and drifting clouds. Below the island is an endless void filled with glowing magical currents. The ground is etched with runes that emit soft light, surrounded by floating stones and mystical plants. The image is in a painterly fantasy style, with rich brushstrokes, dramatic lighting, and an epic, mystical atmosphere.

特点：人物造型俊美，色彩通透柔和，背景略虚化，光效梦幻，常用于小说插图与视觉图。

On a rainy night in the city, a teenage boy in a dark gray trench coat stands quietly on the sidewalk, hands in his pockets, his head slightly lowered as he gazes at the ground. His expression is calm and introspective. Rain blurs the glow of streetlights into soft halos, and neon reflections ripple in the puddles. The background is gently out of focus, with faint silhouettes of signs and passersby. The illustration is in a Japanese light novel style, with soft lighting and a quiet, slightly melancholic atmosphere.
